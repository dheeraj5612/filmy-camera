**Subtitle:** Film looks. Your frame.
**Promotional text:** Choose a film look, compare the original, then shoot. Camera shots save automatically. Tune imported photos on your device and save a new copy.

**Feature description for this pass:**

A quieter camera with direct Tune and Original controls. Compare before you shoot without changing the selected film look. Browse an image-led look library, save favorites, and adjust exposure with explicit step and reset controls. Imported photos support an aligned original/look comparison and optional Instant Print borders. Your original import stays unchanged.

Camera access is requested at use. Camera captures save automatically to Photos; imported edits save only when you tap Save to Photos. Filmy processes looks on device. System Photos/iCloud settings still apply. Original means the camera or imported image before the Filmy look, not sensor RAW.
